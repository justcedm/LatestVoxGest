# Limitations and Ethics

## Technical Limitations

Recognition may be affected by:

- landmark dropout
- hand collision/occlusion
- framing
- lighting
- distance
- signing speed
- camera/device differences
- handedness mapping
- differences between training video and live Android capture

The project must report such limitations rather than hiding them.

## Linguistic Limitations

Developer-performed gestures and team-created animations are not automatically authoritative FSL.

Qualified FSL validation is required before an experimental motion is described as linguistically correct.

## Privacy

Evaluation should follow:

- informed consent
- voluntary participation
- right to stop/withdraw
- anonymized/coded survey records where appropriate
- explicit consent before collecting identifiable photographs or video
- academic/research-only use of participant information

## Appropriate Positioning

VoxGest is an assistive communication prototype.

It should not be presented as a replacement for qualified interpreters in legal, medical, emergency, or other high-stakes communication.

## Research Integrity

The team should transparently distinguish:

- dataset-provided ground truth
- researcher processing/training
- runtime engineering
- candidate features
- verified features
- known errors
- incomplete work
