The lab now offers `CONTROLLED_WINDOW` alongside its preserved MANUAL/AUTO modes. START SIGNING gives a 2,000 ms GET READY interval, then starts a 4,500 ms SIGN NOW capture, automatically ends with `CONTROLLED_WINDOW_END`, and saves/infers without waiting for hand disappearance or the legacy `EVENT_TIMEOUT`. END is disabled during this fixed window; leaving the activity records `LIFECYCLE_STOP`. One START produces at most one saved event. The operator's intended label is snapped before the countdown.

The existing MediaPipe Tasks extractor still uses the unmirrored front-camera analysis and anatomical hand slots. No synthetic duplicate frame or display-interpolated landmark enters inference. The diagnostic gate remains confidence .95, margin .05, motion .02, minimum eight envelope observations, maximum 8,000 ms, pose .65, and any-hand .65. A controlled end is not a timeout rejection; all other quality/gate checks still apply.
